## UTMB
Outside work, Rob is training for UTMB, Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc, 171km through the Alps.

## Training engine
Rob built an LLM-powered training engine for it. It ingests his Strava activity data, chunks and embeds it, retrieves relevant training history through a vector search layer (RAG), and generates adaptive weekly plans from recent load, fatigue and the race timeline. It has a feedback loop where he flags what worked. It is his most-used personal project.
