Rob started in 2021 at J.P. Morgan Dublin as a Software Engineer, building backend services for internal financial systems in Java and Spring Boot. It was a regulated environment where reliability and auditability were not optional.

He mentored two junior engineers as the team scaled. He was promoted to Senior Software Engineer in February 2026.
