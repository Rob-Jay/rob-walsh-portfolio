## Who Rob is
Rob Walsh is a Senior Software Engineer based in Dublin, Ireland. He has five years of experience building production backend systems in financial services, in Java, Spring Boot and microservices at scale, at Mastercard and J.P. Morgan.

## Direction
Rob wants to work on AI itself: integrating AI services into products, engineering the systems that connect models to the real world, and building the tooling around them. He wants the engineering work to be the AI, not a feature on the roadmap.
